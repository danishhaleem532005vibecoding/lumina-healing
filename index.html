Privacy Policy

Lumina Healing is committed to respecting and protecting the privacy of everyone who contacts us or visits our website.

Information We Collect

When you contact us via WhatsApp, phone, or our online enquiry form, we collect the basic details you provide (such as your name, email address, phone number, and message enquiry) solely to communicate with you regarding your wellbeing enquiry.

How We Use Your Information

Your details are used exclusively to respond to your queries, discuss prospective appointments, and provide relevant information about our services. We do not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties for commercial or marketing purposes.

Confidentiality

All consultations and discussions with Ellen Hanley are treated with utmost respect, discretion, and confidentiality in accordance with professional ethical standards.

Your Rights

Under Irish and European Data Protection regulations (GDPR), you have the right to request access to any personal data we hold about you, or ask for your details to be updated or deleted. To make a request, please contact Ellen Hanley directly at +353 87 366 2435.
